# The Living Arcade + CardMaker tour

Animated banner for CardMaker, Brian's card maker for Anbernic retro handhelds, plus a click-through tour.

Live page: https://bcooney2026.github.io/living-arcade/

- Banner: tap the buttons under it for a flyby, the Donkey Kong and Pac-Man scenes, or the rare Easter egg.
- Take the tour: CardMaker's five home-screen buttons. Click one to step through what it shows on the MacBook.
  Screens are real CardMaker screenshots (1440x900, Retina) made from Brian's own library data.
- tour/tour.js lists the flows and captions; tour/screens and tour/tiles hold the images.
- Pixel font: Press Start 2P (SIL Open Font License, tour/fonts/OFL.txt).

Fan art: the game characters belong to their respective owners.
